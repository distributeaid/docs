## Save your progress

You can save an entry before all information is available and return
to complete it later. Be sure to save your progress as you work.

Saving an entry does not publish it to the website. Strapi saves it as a **Draft** entry.

Required fields may remain incomplete when saving. Strapi checks
these requirements when you try to publish, so saving successfully
does not mean the entry is ready for publication.

### Save an incomplete entry

1. Enter the information currently available.
2. Select Save.
3. Confirm that the entry has been saved as a **Draft**.
4. Return to the entry when you have the remaining information.

Before publishing, complete the remaining fields and review the
[Before publishing checklist](#before-publishing).

## Before publishing

Complete this checklist when the entry is ready for publication.
You do not need to complete it just to save your progress.

- All required fields are complete.
- Content has been checked for accuracy, spelling, and approved wording.
- Links point to the correct destinations.
- Images are correct and meet the field requirements.
- Repeated items are in the intended order.
- No placeholder or test content remains.
- The content has received the required approval.

[Continue to Publish and check the website](#publish-and-check-the-website)

## Publish and check the website

Publish only when the entry is complete and approved. Only published
entries appear on the website.

1. Complete the [Before publishing checklist](#before-publishing).
2. Select Publish.
3. If Strapi displays validation warnings, correct the identified
   fields and try publishing again.
4. Confirm that the entry is **Published**.
5. Check the content on the website after the expected update process
   has completed.
